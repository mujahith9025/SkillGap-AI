AI Interview Feedback
Simple, friendly tips to help you ace your interview.
Score: 8 / 10 (Good)
✅ Key Concepts Covered:
💡 Points to Remember:
💪 What You Did Well: Accurately explained WHERE row filtering before aggregation.
🎯 How to Make It Even Better: Mention how GROUP BY groups rows together.
🎯 Recommended Model Response (STAR Framework):
Situation & Task: In SQL pipelines, WHERE filters individual rows before grouping, while HAVING filters aggregated metric results.
Action & Example: For example, `WHERE salary > 50000` filters employees before aggregation. Conversely, `HAVING COUNT(*) > 5` filters grouped departments after aggregation, which cannot be done with WHERE because aggregates don't exist prior to grouping.
Result: This ensures correct query pipeline execution and optimal indexing.
